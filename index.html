🌍 About Me

I am someone who believes every question opens a new door. Whether it’s about Tableau dashboards, project management frameworks, digital strategies, or entrepreneurship models, my curiosity drives me to explore, connect, and simplify.

🔎 My journey is not just about finding answers — it’s about asking the right questions:
✅ How can data inspire decisions, not just reports?
✅ How do we balance technology with human insight?
✅ What makes a business truly scalable in today’s digital-first world?
✅ How can learning be made simple, fast, and practical?

💡 These questions shape me as a Tableau Coach, guiding professionals and students to not just use a tool, but to think with data. They also define me as an Entrepreneur, where I blend 20+ years of industry experience with the vision to help businesses, startups, and individuals thrive in the digital ecosystem.

🚀 Whether through analytics, coaching, or digital ventures, my purpose is clear: to turn curiosity into clarity, data into stories, and challenges into opportunities.

✨ At heart, I’m not just a professional. I’m a curious mind, a teacher at soul, and a builder of possibilities.
